CA certificate bundle

Chaquopy 17.2.0 supplies cacert.pem from certifi/Mozilla's certificate store.
The upstream release changelog identifies certifi 2026.10.23. The CA bundle
is retained unchanged for runtime initialization. Its Mozilla Public License
2.0 notice is in certifi-LICENSE.txt and the full license is in MPL-2.0.txt.
The latter contains only the MPL text from Mozilla's official NSS COPYING
document; the NSS runtime itself is not bundled by this application.
Corresponding certificate source is available from certifi's source releases
and https://github.com/certifi/python-certifi, and from Mozilla's certificate
store at https://hg.mozilla.org/mozilla-central/file/tip/security/nss/lib/ckfw/builtins/certdata.txt.
